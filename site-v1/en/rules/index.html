Community rules

The same rules as on our Discord server. They also apply to what you send through this site: bug reports and applications.

  1. Be kind.

    Respect everyone, whatever their level. No insults, harassment, hate or discrimination.

  2. Use the right channel.

    Questions go in the help channel, bugs in the bugs forum, ideas in the ideas channel.

  3. No spam or self-promotion.

    No ads, no invite links, no mass mentions.

  4. No piracy, cracks or cheats.

    We don't help anyone get around a license or an anti-cheat.

  5. Keep it safe for work.

    No adult, violent or shocking content.

  6. Protect your privacy.

    Never post a password, a recovery key or personal details, not even in a screenshot.

  7. English or French.

    Chat in English or French. In the forums, either language is fine.

  8. Follow Discord's rules.

    Its Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, published on discord.com, apply too.

What moderators do

  • They can remove anything that breaks these rules.
  • Usually: a reminder first, a timeout next, removal last. A serious breach can lead straight to removal.
  • If something bothers you, mention a moderator or open a ticket on the server.

Appealing a decision

Write to bonjour@rayelinux.com and say what happened. Someone on the team other than the person who decided reviews the decision whenever possible.
