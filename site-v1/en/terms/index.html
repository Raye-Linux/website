Raye is a beta

The current version is a public beta. It can have bugs, crash, not work with your hardware and, in rare cases, lose data. In particular:

  • installing can erase the whole disk you pick; installing next to another system means resizing partitions, which always carries a risk;
  • updates can change or remove features, and a beta update may require reinstalling;
  • apps from other systems may not all work.

Back up your files before you install, check the downloaded image with the checksum and signature given on the Download page, and don't use the beta where a failure could hurt someone or cause serious damage (medical equipment, safety, industrial control).

Downloading the beta asks you to tick a box that repeats these warnings; it doesn't replace these terms, it reminds you of them.
