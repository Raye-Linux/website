The project

The releases, from 0.1 to 1.0

Before 1.0, Raye only ran on my test machines: no image was published, and the betas reached them through Raye Update, the way updates will reach you. Here is what each one brought.

  1. 1.0

    to come

    The Clock and Graphics Card apps, reviews in the Raye Store, Try Raye without installing, a Setup that keeps your files, and a more reliable Raye Update.

  2. 0.5 Beta

    November 14, 2026

    The first image anyone can download. Archives in File Explorer, disk encryption from Settings, a filled Control Panel, and fixes: the flicker of the Performance page, a see-through menu in Settings, the update screen off-center on two screens, Wi-Fi shown on a PC that has none.

  3. 0.3 Beta

    November 11, 2026

    Task Manager, a rebuilt File Explorer with its tabs and This PC, Calendar, Control Panel and the Registry Editor. Updates that install without a restart. Calmer colors, six wallpapers, new pointers. On the test machine, the first black screen at startup goes from 18.3 s to 3 s; a second one, 3 to 5 s before the sign-in screen, remains.

  4. 0.2 Beta 2

    November 9, 2026

    Two rounds of fixes on the night of November 8 to 9, less than five hours apart. The second adds the piece that was missing to update the BIOS of a Dell PC, hides a battery estimate when it goes past 24 hours and straightens the Raye Update icon.

  5. 0.2 Beta

    November 8, 2026

    The first packages published. The installer got its own Disk page, which reads the disk again before writing. A French session finally gets French folder names. A minimized window keeps its taskbar button.

  6. The website

    October 31, 2026

    rayelinux.com opens, with a “coming soon” page and a signup list.

  7. 0.1

    October 30, 2026

    The first working image, never published: enough to install, start and begin measuring.
