What the role is

  • You translate the texts of Raye's apps and of this site, from English.
  • You keep the words Raye uses the same everywhere (Start, Settings, File Explorer).
  • You check the translation in Raye itself, for texts that are too long or cut off.

What we ask

  • Writing the target language like your everyday language.
  • Reading English well.
  • Simple sentences, no jargon: Raye is made for people who are not technicians.

Every application is read by the Raye Team. Nothing is accepted automatically.
