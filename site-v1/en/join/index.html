Join the team

Help make Raye.

Raye is made by one person, with help from its community. If you want to test, translate, moderate, write code or draw, pick a role, read what it asks for, then send a short application.

The roles

  • Beta tester

    Install the private betas before they ship and say what breaks.

    Read and apply

  • Public beta tester

    Switch to the public beta and talk about it in its own channel.

    Read and apply

  • Beta tester Dev

    The development builds, which can break, for people who are at ease.

    Read and apply

  • Discord moderator

    Keep the Discord server welcoming and useful.

    Read and apply

  • Translator

    Put Raye and this site in a new language, or proofread the French.

    Read and apply

  • Developer

    Fix bugs and write code in the public repositories.

    Read and apply

  • Design and more

    Icons, wallpapers, sounds, guides, videos: whatever you're good at.

    Read and apply

How it works

  1. You send the form

    Two minutes: your Discord name, an address to answer you, and a few sentences on what you want to do.

  2. The Raye Team reads it

    A person on the team reads every application. Nothing is accepted automatically. We try to answer within two weeks.

  3. You get your role

    If it's a yes, you get the role on our Discord server, with its private channel. For the testing roles, access to the matching update channel will follow when those channels open.

  4. You leave whenever you like

    Say so on Discord or by email: the role is removed and your application is deleted.

What's in it for you

  • You see what's new before everyone else, and your opinion changes what ships.
  • You talk with the Raye Team directly, in a private channel.
  • Nothing to pay, no contract: it's volunteering, at your own pace.
