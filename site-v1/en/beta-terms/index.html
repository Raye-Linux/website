Beta program terms

Last updated: November 20, 2026 · version 4.0

Version prepared for the V1 site. It will apply when that site goes online; until then, version 3.0 published on rayelinux.com is the one in force.

In short

  • Three levels: public beta for whoever asks; private and Dev beta after the Raye Team accepts you.
  • A beta can break: keep a copy of your files, no Dev beta on your only work PC.
  • No warranty, as far as the law allows; your consumer rights stay.
  • No confidentiality agreement; if the team asks you to keep something to yourself, it says so.
  • You leave whenever you like.

The program

The beta program lets you try Raye before its final versions. It has three levels: the public beta, open to whoever asks; the private beta, and the Dev beta, kept for people the Raye Team accepts after an application on Join the team.

Today, being accepted gives a role and a channel on our Discord server. The matching update channels in Raye Update are planned and not open yet.

Software under test

A beta is not finished. It can crash, misbehave with your hardware, or need Raye to be reinstalled. The Dev beta breaks more often than the others.

  • Keep a copy of your important files somewhere other than the test PC.
  • Don't install a private or Dev beta on the only PC you need for work.
  • Raye's restore points help, but don't replace a copy.

No warranty

Betas are provided as they are, with no warranty that they work or suit a given use, as far as the law allows. The "Warranties" and "Liability" sections of the terms of use apply. Nothing here removes the rights the law gives you as a consumer.

Your reports and ideas

The reports, screenshots and ideas you share are used to fix and improve Raye. You let us use them freely, without payment. You remain the author of what you write. Don't put a password, a recovery key or information about another person in a report.

What hasn't shipped yet

Nothing in the program is secret by default, and there is no confidentiality agreement to sign. When the team asks you to keep a version or a feature to yourself until it ships, it says so clearly in the channel concerned, and we count on you to respect it. Not doing so can lead to the role being removed.

Leaving, or losing access

  • You can leave the program at any time: say so on Discord or at bonjour@rayelinux.com.
  • The team can remove access if the community rules or these terms are not followed, or after a long absence.
  • The program can change or stop; important changes are announced on Discord.

Your information

What the application keeps, for how long and how to have it deleted is in the privacy policy, section "Applications to join the team".

Contact

A question about these terms: bonjour@rayelinux.com.
