The project

What version 1.0 contains

This page follows the roadmap: every line comes from its V1 section, which I keep up to date. A line is written here when the change is made; what I couldn't try on a real PC is said apart, at the bottom.

Installing and starting

  • The USB stick starts on the Raye trial, with an Install icon on the desktop. The installer opens full screen. Details are on the Install page.
  • The startup menu is in English only, and several installer flaws are fixed: the misplaced Accessibility button, the Wi-Fi field, the copy window that stayed on “Finishing writing”.
  • A Raye kernel, which replaces the base system's: community patches for responsiveness, memory and sleep, plus Raye's own tuning. Switching from one to the other asks you to enroll a key once, on a blue screen at restart (Install page).

Games and graphics cards

  • Steam sees your other drives: whatever is mounted under /media, /run/media and /mnt. Proved in the test machine, for Steam installed from the Store.
  • A fix for older AMD cards (RX 400 and 500), which stayed in low resolution or on a black screen, and one for the black flicker of NVIDIA cards on a 144 Hz screen.
  • Raye is ready for Easy Anti-Cheat v2: its kernel's key was sent to Epic. It's all on the Games page.

Memory and stability

  • The same memory at idle on every PC, about 850 MB, even with a lot of RAM. Before, the kernel reserved more of it the more the machine had.
  • No more freezes when memory runs out: the PC slows down a little instead.
  • Unneeded services removed and background tuning, for a lighter and more stable Raye.
  • The Electron apps that crashed on start (Qoder, among others) start.

Files, network and apps

  • File Explorer opens right away, even when a network drive doesn't answer.
  • The copy window to a USB stick shows the real speed, with its Minimize and Close buttons.
  • The Wi-Fi password field in quick settings keeps the cursor while the list of networks refreshes.
  • A WireGuard VPN in one click: import a file, turn it on from quick settings.
  • Info Center opens Raye's Task Manager and takes its look.
  • The Windows programs page also lists the ones installed from the Store.
  • The Store takes APT packages first, Flatpak only when there is no APT one, and accepts AppImage. The apps you have as Flatpak move to APT once, with nothing lost.

The desktop and its look

  • Raye's new logo, in the system, the installer, the website and Discord, with a new startup animation.
  • Taskbar settings: left or center, icon size, height, auto-hide.
  • Windows that wobble when you move them, with a switch in Settings.

Hyprland

  • A smoother Hyprland session, Settings and Raye apps that work on it, and a complete guide. It's all on the Two desktops page.

What is still missing

  • Part of V1 is still in progress and isn't listed above: the Raye account and the Raye Community beta, Raye Proton, more hardware that works out of the box, the Printers & scanners page, the encrypted-disk unlock screen, the complete Windows bridge, the taskbar on top or on the side. Each line moves here when it is done; the roadmap says where they stand.
  • The fixes for older AMD cards and for NVIDIA cards were not tried on a real card: the test machine has no graphics card.
  • Older PCs (BIOS-mode start, processors such as the Core 2 Duo, 2 to 4 GB of memory) are still being tried. See the Older PCs page.
  • Raye's kernel hasn't yet been tried on a real PC with Secure Boot, or with a real NVIDIA card, and hibernation is blocked while Secure Boot is on.
  • I'm still going through every screen in French, installer included.
  • The WireGuard VPN and the taskbar settings were seen in the test machine; I haven't seen the wobbling windows yet, which the test machine's rendering can't show.
