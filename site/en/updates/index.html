Updates

On November 11, 2026, three updates came in on the test machine. The Store and Calendar installed while the session was open; the desktop theme waited for the restart.

What installs right away, what waits

What waits for the restart is what the system keeps loaded while it runs: the kernel, drivers, firmware, the core pieces of the system and the desktop itself. An update of that kind carries the label “Restart required”. The rest installs as soon as it's downloaded, with nothing for you to do.

Security fixes are the exception: they install right away, even for those pieces.

The restart

What waits installs when you choose “Update and restart”, in Start, in the notification or in Settings. An ordinary restart installs nothing, and Raye doesn't restart on its own unless you asked it to outside your active hours. While it installs, the Raye picture shows a ring and “Installing updates” with the percentage. For the desktop theme, 24 s passed between the startup picture and the sign-in screen on the test machine.

What is still missing

  • When a second update of the same package is waiting, the “Restart required” notification doesn't come back.
  • The lock screen and Ctrl+Alt+Del don't offer “Update and restart” yet.

When Raye downloads, and when it waits

Updates download in the background and wait while your laptop is on battery below 40%, on a metered connection or playing a game. You can pause everything for one to five weeks, except security. Checking for and downloading 42 updates, 60 MB in all, took 22 s on the test machine.

What is still missing

  • Below 40% battery, “Install now” does nothing, and shows nothing.

Restore points

Before and after every update, Raye takes a restore point of the system, and the oldest go by themselves. You can also make one in Settings, Recovery.

What is still missing

  • Going back to the “Before updates” point reinstalls the same updates at the next start: don't count on it yet to undo an update.
  • A point made by hand can be deleted, with no warning, by those of the following updates.
  • A restore point keeps the system, not your personal files. Back them up separately.
  • You can't yet start from an earlier state through the startup menu.

Where updates come from

Raye's packages come from dl.rayelinux.com and raye-linux.github.io/packages, two copies identical file by file, signed by the key whose fingerprint is EA73 69D4 C3A3 10B2 DF3D FC16 D3AA 2E62 5DDE 161E. Firefox comes from Mozilla, and the rest of the system from the base system's servers; if the main one stops answering, Raye moves to two others by itself.

What is still missing

  • Without a connection, Raye Update says it could not check: it didn't reach the servers, nothing is broken.
