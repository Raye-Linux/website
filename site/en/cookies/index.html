Cookies and local storage

Last updated: November 26, 2026 · version 4.0

Version prepared for the V1 site. It will apply when that site goes online; until then, version 3.0 published on rayelinux.com is the one in force. These pages are written by the Raye team in plain words; they were not reviewed by a lawyer.

In short

  • The site uses no cookies and does not measure its audience.
  • Only your language can stay on your device, if you pick one.
  • The accounts site and Raye Community each set a session cookie; the accounts site also a language cookie if you pick one.

Contents

  1. What the site sets today
  2. What stays in your browser
  3. Other services
  4. The accounts site and Raye Community
  5. Raye Linux itself

What the site sets today

rayelinux.com uses no cookies, no pixels, and no third-party scripts or fonts, and does not measure its audience. There is nothing to accept or refuse: no consent banner is needed.

What stays in your browser

WhatWhereWhyHow long
Your language ("raye-lang", "fr" or "en")local storage, your device onlyopen the right language; never sent to the serveruntil you clear the site's data
The help bubble, the download page's tick box, the application formthe open page onlywork while you are on the pageuntil you close the page

To clear your language, clear the site's data in your browser.

Other services

Cloudflare serves the pages and the ISO file (see the privacy policy). It tries to add an audience measurement script to the pages; the site's security policy blocks it, so it never runs. Discord, GitHub, Codeberg and GitLab, where some links lead, have their own cookies and rules: they only apply once you are there.

The accounts site and Raye Community

The Raye accounts site (account.rayelinux.com) sets one session cookie, which keeps you signed in for 14 days at most, and, if you pick a language, one cookie that remembers it for a year; no other.

Raye Community (community.rayelinux.com) sets one session cookie, 30 days at most, and, while you sign in with your Raye account, one check cookie that lasts 10 minutes; no other.

The team panel keeps the signed-in person's token in the tab's storage (sessionStorage), never in a cookie; it is gone when the tab closes.

Raye Linux itself

The system sends no usage or crash reports. The browser (Firefox) you use in it has its own cookies, as on any system.
