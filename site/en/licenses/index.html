Raye Linux is free software

You may use it for any purpose, study, share and change it, under the licenses of its parts. Those licenses give you these freedoms directly: you don't need to ask me anything.

What the GPL lets you do, and asks of you

  • You may run the program for any purpose, private or commercial.
  • You may study and change it, which means having its source code.
  • You may share copies, changed or not, for free or for a fee.
  • If you share a copy, you must pass on the same freedoms: the same license and access to the matching source code.

These licenses come with no warranty; see also the terms of use.

Getting the source code

To get the source code that matches the version of Raye you use, write to bonjour@rayelinux.com with the version (it is in Settings, System, About). I give it to you at no cost as a download, for at least three years after that version was last distributed. The code of the base system's packages is also published by their authors.

Contributions

A bug report, idea or fix you share stays yours. A code fix you send me to go into Raye is accepted under the license of the part it changes.

Trademarks

"Raye Linux", "Raye" in the names of its tools, and the Raye eye are our own. They are not under a free license, so that nobody is misled about what comes from us.

What you can do without asking

  • Talk about Raye, review it, make a tutorial or a video about it, and show the logo to illustrate it.
  • Share unchanged copies of the official image, under its name.
  • Say a product "works with Raye Linux", if it is true.

What needs my consent

  • Sharing a changed version under Raye's name or logo: give it another name and logo, and you may say it is "based on Raye Linux".
  • Using the name or logo in a way that suggests I endorse you, or in a domain, app or account name that could be mistaken for the project.
