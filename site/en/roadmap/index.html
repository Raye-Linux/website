Roadmap

What's next for Raye Linux. Ideas from the community come first: share yours in #ideas on our Discord.

The #roadmap channel on the Discord has the same list.

V1

In progress · end of November

12 of 33 done
  • Done: Steam sees your other drives
  • In progress: Fix for older AMD cards (RX 400/500) stuck on a black screen or low resolution
  • In progress: Fix for the flicker on NVIDIA cards at 144 Hz
  • Done: Same low RAM use at idle on every PC (about 850 MB), even with lots of RAM
  • In progress: No more freezes when RAM runs out: the PC slows down a little instead
  • Done: Fix for apps that crash on start (Qoder and other Electron apps)
  • In progress: Wi-Fi password box keeps the cursor while networks refresh
  • Done: File Explorer opens right away, even when a network drive is out of reach
  • In progress: Windows programs page lists the ones installed from the Store
  • In progress: Smoother Hyprland
  • In progress: Unneeded services removed and global optimizations, for a lighter and more stable Raye
  • In progress: Website refresh: a cleaner, more professional look and more pages
  • In progress: Taskbar settings: left or center, icon size, taskbar height, auto-hide
  • Done: Customize Hyprland your way: your own settings file, simple instructions, never overwritten
  • In progress: USB copies show the real speed, with close and minimize buttons
  • Done: Store apps you have move from Flatpak to APT once, nothing lost
  • Done: Store: APT apps first, Flatpak only when not on APT, plus AppImage
  • In progress: WireGuard VPN in one click: import a file, turn it on from Quick Settings
  • Done: Info Center opens Raye's Task Manager and looks like Raye
  • In progress: Wobbly windows: a switch in Settings
  • In progress: Settings and every Raye app work well on Hyprland
  • In progress: Complete Hyprland guide: change everything, Raye apps included, step by step
  • Done: Install other Hyprland setups (dotfiles like Caelestia) in one step, and go back to Raye's anytime
  • In progress: New Raye logo everywhere: system, installer, website, Discord
  • Done: New boot animation with the new logo
  • Done: New Raye kernel: CachyOS and XanMod patches plus Raye's own tuning
  • Done: Raye kernel only (the stock kernel is removed)
  • In progress: Ready for Easy Anti-Cheat v2 (Fortnite): Raye's kernel key sent to Epic
  • Planned: Works on older PCs too (legacy BIOS, older processors like Core 2 Duo, 2–4 GB RAM)
  • In progress: New install: start in Try Raye with an Install icon, full-screen Setup, English boot menu, Setup fixes
  • Planned: Everything checked in French, screen by screen (installer included)
  • In progress: Final polish and fixes from both betas
  • Planned: New ISO for everyone

V1.1

In progress · early December

0 of 15 done
  • In progress: Windows apps install silently with a real progress bar, start to finish
  • Planned: Taskbar on top or on the side of the screen
  • Planned: Raye Community beta: Raye's own chat, on the website and in Raye Linux, with your Raye account, roles, moderation and friends
  • Planned: The V1 website, with fresh screenshots of the real system
  • Planned: Legal pages and FAQ
  • Planned: Join the team and a staff panel on the website
  • Planned: Public profiles and badges
  • Planned: More hardware works out of the box: Realtek and other Wi-Fi, webcam and sound drivers installed on their own when a device needs them
  • Planned: Quadra in the Store (AppImage), with game mode on while Roblox runs
  • Planned: Raye account: your settings follow you to every PC
  • Planned: New account website and emails, with two-factor sign-in
  • Planned: Raye Proton: pick Raye's Windows layer for Steam games
  • Planned: Printers & scanners page and a Scan app, plus longer laptop battery life
  • Planned: A real Raye screen to unlock an encrypted disk at startup
  • In progress: Complete Windows bridge: share drivers and settings with Windows
