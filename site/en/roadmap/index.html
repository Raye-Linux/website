Done · end of November

  • Done: Same low RAM use at idle on every PC (about 850 MB), even with lots of RAM

  • Done: Fix for apps that crash on start (Qoder and other Electron apps)
  • Done: Wi-Fi password box keeps the cursor while networks refresh
  • Done: File Explorer opens right away, even when a network drive is out of reach
  • Done: Windows programs page lists the ones installed from the Store
  • Done: Smoother Hyprland
  • Done: Unneeded services removed and global optimizations, for a lighter and more stable Raye
  • Done: Website refresh: a cleaner, more professional look and more pages
  • Done: Taskbar settings: left or center, icon size, taskbar height, auto-hide
  • Done: Customize Hyprland your way: your own settings file, simple instructions, never overwritten
  • Done: USB copies show the real speed, with close and minimize buttons
  • Done: Store apps you have move from Flatpak to APT once, nothing lost
  • Done: Store: APT apps first, Flatpak only when not on APT, plus AppImage
  • Done: WireGuard VPN in one click: import a file, turn it on from Quick Settings
  • Done: Info Center opens Raye's Task Manager and looks like Raye
  • Done: Wobbly windows: a switch in Settings
  • Done: Settings and every Raye app work well on Hyprland
  • Done: Complete Hyprland guide: change everything, Raye apps included, step by step
  • Done: Install other Hyprland setups (dotfiles like Caelestia) in one step, and go back to Raye's anytime
  • Done: Windows apps install silently with a real progress bar, start to finish
  • Done: Printers & scanners page and a Scan app, plus longer laptop battery life
  • Done: A real Raye screen to unlock an encrypted disk at startup
  • Done: Complete Windows bridge: Windows programs see your drives, printers, network and ports
  • Done: Taskbar on top or on the side of the screen
  • Done: Raye Community beta: Raye's own chat, on the website and in Raye Linux, with your Raye account, roles, moderation and friends
  • Done: The V1 website, with fresh screenshots of the real system
  • Done: Legal pages and FAQ
  • Done: Join the team and a staff panel on the website
  • Done: Public profiles and badges
  • Done: More hardware works out of the box: Realtek and other Wi-Fi, webcam and sound drivers installed on their own when a device needs them
  • Done: Quadra in the Store (AppImage), with game mode on while Roblox runs
  • Done: Raye account: your settings follow you to every PC
  • Done: New account website and emails, with two-factor sign-in
  • Done: Raye Proton: pick Raye's Windows layer for Steam games
  • Done: Raye Proton built on GE-Proton, with per-game fixes from Raye Update
  • Done: Upgraded Windows layer: newest version, faster, more Windows apps tested and working
  • Done: New Raye logo everywhere: system, installer, website, Discord
  • Done: New boot animation with the new logo
  • Done: New Raye kernel: CachyOS and XanMod patches plus Raye's own tuning
  • Done: Raye kernel only (the stock kernel is removed)
  • Done: Ready for Easy Anti-Cheat v2 (Fortnite): Raye's kernel key sent to Epic
  • Done: Works on older PCs too (legacy BIOS, older processors like Core 2 Duo, 2–4 GB RAM)
  • Done: New install: start in Try Raye with an Install icon, full-screen Setup, English boot menu, Setup fixes
  • Done: Everything checked in French, screen by screen (installer included)
  • Done: Raye in Spanish, German, Brazilian Portuguese and Italian
  • Done: Raye Insider in Settings: Public Beta (sign up on the website), Beta and Dev update channels (application, access tied to your Raye account), leave anytime
  • Done: Accounts: badges, community and admin panel
  • Done: Developer options and remote web page
  • Done: Final polish and fixes from both betas
  • Done: New ISO for everyone

V1.1

In progress · early December

0 of 15 done
  • Planned: Taskbars on several screens show the same apps in the same order
  • Planned: Community idea: bigger icons in Start, with a size setting
  • Planned: Community idea: more blur, like Windows' acrylic, on the taskbar, Start, menus and panels
  • Planned: Community idea: a lock screen you can customize (picture, clock, what it shows)
  • Planned: Community idea: app icons closer to the Windows 11 look, drawn by Raye
  • In progress: Fix for the flicker on NVIDIA cards at 144 Hz
  • In progress: Fix for older AMD cards (RX 400/500) stuck on a black screen or low resolution
  • Planned: Welcome app after install: quick setup (drivers, updates, apps, Raye account, look) and where to get help
  • Planned: Raye Community complete, built on Matrix: end-to-end encryption everywhere (your Raye account handles the keys), images and videos, private messages, the same channels as the Discord server and support tickets, a layout like Discord's with older messages as you scroll, who's online and a status (online, idle, do not disturb, invisible), your Raye profile picture, its own right-click menu, bots and commands with Raye Assistant answering, staff tools, then it replaces the Discord server
  • Planned: Every language: all of Raye translated
  • In progress: 40+ popular Windows apps in the Store, each one tested, and "Optimized for Raye" badges
  • Planned: Forgot your password? Reset it from the sign-in screen, with a recovery key made in Setup or with your Raye account
  • Planned: Taskbar: a transparency slider and an apps-only taskbar
  • Planned: Join the team: links to your past work in the form, a starting trust score that grows with your work, emails at each step; accepting someone gives their Discord role, then access step by step (a safe test mirror, GitHub, the server mirror), and new developers' changes go to the Dev channel for testers
  • Planned: Raye Team panel: new moderators and team members start on probation, then get full access and their own staff key by email (Raye account plus key to sign in); people already in the team are added and told by private message
