V1

Done · end of November

1 of 1 done
  • Done: Raye Linux 1.0 is out, with the 1.0.1 and 1.0.2 updates: everything it brought is in #release-notes and on rayelinux.com/en/whats-new

V1.1

In progress · early December

1 of 29 done
  • Planned: Taskbars on several screens show the same apps in the same order
  • Planned: Community idea: bigger icons in Start, with a size setting
  • Planned: Community idea: more blur, like Windows' acrylic, on the taskbar, Start, menus and panels
  • Planned: Community idea: a lock screen you can customize (picture, clock, what it shows)
  • Planned: Community idea: app icons closer to the Windows 11 look, drawn by Raye
  • Planned: Community idea: Horizon (Minecraft Java rewritten in Rust, faster) in the Store, with Game Mode while you play
  • Planned: Community idea: DankMaterialShell (DMS) as a one-click setup for Hyprland, and for niri and MangoWC once Raye offers them
  • In progress: Fix for the flicker on NVIDIA cards at 144 Hz
  • In progress: Fix for older AMD cards (RX 400/500) stuck on a black screen or low resolution
  • Done: Fix for the sign-in screen that shows only its background on some PCs (it still works when you type your password)
  • Planned: Fix: a Windows program installed by double-click always shows up in Start and as installed in the Store, even if the PC went to sleep or was turned off during setup, and its space is never lost
  • Planned: FL Studio runs great on Raye: tested in the lab, its own recipe, low latency, and Open in the Store launches FL Studio itself, not one of its plugins
  • Planned: Fix: Windows apps from the Store are checked after install, so a missing file (like FL Studio's "QuickFontCache DLL not found") is caught and repaired by itself, for every app
  • Planned: Fix: Bluetooth devices with no screen or code (adapters, headphones) pair without asking for a PIN or saying the code does not match
  • Planned: Fix: choosing a file from a browser or an app ("Select file") opens Raye's File Explorer without turning the window behind it grey
  • Planned: Welcome app after install: quick setup (drivers, updates, apps, Raye account, look) and where to get help
  • Planned: Raye Community complete, built on Matrix: end-to-end encryption everywhere (your Raye account handles the keys), images and videos, private messages, the same channels as the Discord server and support tickets, a layout like Discord's with older messages as you scroll, who's online and a status (online, idle, do not disturb, invisible), your Raye profile picture, its own right-click menu, bots and commands with Raye Assistant answering, staff tools, then it replaces the Discord server
  • Planned: Every language: all of Raye translated
  • Planned: Raye WinLayer: Raye's Windows layer, faster with ntsync, a ready recipe for each tested app (components installed by themselves), and a diagnosis that suggests a fix when an app crashes
  • Planned: An automated lab that installs and tests Windows apps on its own, compares them with Windows and fixes what breaks
  • Planned: Raye WinLayer engine: new parts built by Raye on the ReactOS foundation take over step by step, first for console tools and small apps
  • Planned: Raye WinLayer tuned for Raye: the Raye kernel's speedups, lab-tested recipes sent to every Raye PC, and graphics drivers already in place
  • In progress: 40+ popular Windows apps in the Store, each one tested, and "Optimized for Raye" badges
  • Planned: Windows programs: a Raye window with a progress bar when one gets ready, showing each step live (preparing, installing, setting up) instead of a single notification
  • Planned: Forgot your password? Reset it from the sign-in screen, with a recovery key made in Setup or with your Raye account
  • Planned: Taskbar: a transparency slider and an apps-only taskbar
  • Planned: Join the team: links to your past work in the form, a starting trust score that grows with your work, emails at each step; accepting someone gives their Discord role, then access step by step (a safe test mirror, GitHub, the server mirror), and new developers' changes go to the Dev channel for testers
  • Planned: Raye Team panel: new moderators and team members start on probation, then get full access and their own staff key by email (Raye account plus key to sign in); people already in the team are added and told by private message
  • Planned: A Team page on the website: everyone accepted into the Raye team shows up on their own, grouped by role (team, developers, translators, contributors, testers), with their Discord name and picture, or their Raye account's picture

V1.2
