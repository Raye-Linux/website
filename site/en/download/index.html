Check the download

  1. Check that the checksum list really comes from Raye Linux. It must say “Good signature”:

    gpg --import raye-archive.asc
gpg --verify SHA256SUMS.asc SHA256SUMS

  2. Check the image. It must say “OK”:

    sha256sum --check --ignore-missing SHA256SUMS

Make a USB stick

  • Rufus: choose the ISO and, when asked, write it in DD image mode. ISO mode gives a stick that doesn't start.
  • balenaEtcher: choose the ISO, then the stick, then Flash.
  • dd: replace sdX with your stick and check twice with lsblk: everything on it will be erased.
sudo dd if=raye-1.0-beta-amd64.iso of=/dev/sdX bs=4M conv=fsync status=progress

Then start the computer from the stick, often with F12, F11, F8 or Esc at power on.

What you need

  • A 64-bit Intel or AMD processor, starting in UEFI or BIOS mode
  • 4 GB of memory, 8 GB recommended
  • 25 GB of free disk space
  • A USB stick of 8 GB or more
  • Secure Boot and the TPM chip are supported, not required

Known issues in the beta

  • Older AMD cards (RX 400 and 500, like the RX 550): on some PCs they still start at a low resolution with low frame rates. We are working on it. If the screen stays black, pick "Safe graphics" on the USB stick; once installed, Raye switches to a simpler display mode by itself if the desktop fails to show twice in a row.
  • NVIDIA cards: the game speed fix has not been tried by us on a real NVIDIA card yet. If your games are still slow, say so on Discord.
  • The terminal, the screenshot tool and a few dialogs wear Raye's colors but are not redrawn as Raye apps yet.
  • .exe programs: the first start takes a few seconds, and not all of them run.
  • Offline, Settings says updates could not be checked.
