Check the download

  1. Check that the checksum list really comes from Raye Linux. It must say “Good signature”:

    gpg --import raye-archive.asc
gpg --verify SHA256SUMS.asc SHA256SUMS

  2. Check the image. It must say “OK”:

    sha256sum --check --ignore-missing SHA256SUMS

Make a USB stick

  • Rufus: choose the ISO and, when asked, write it in DD image mode. ISO mode gives a stick that doesn't start.
  • balenaEtcher: choose the ISO, then the stick, then Flash.
  • dd: replace sdX with your stick and check twice with lsblk: everything on it will be erased.
sudo dd if=raye-1.0-amd64.iso of=/dev/sdX bs=4M conv=fsync status=progress

Then start the computer from the stick, often with F12, F11, F8 or Esc at power on. Install.

What you need

  • A 64-bit Intel or AMD processor and a PC that starts in UEFI mode or the older BIOS mode (MBR disks too)
  • 8 GB of memory recommended
  • 25 GB of free disk space
  • A USB stick of 8 GB or more
  • Secure Boot and a TPM chip are supported, not required

What to know

  • On the first start under Secure Boot, moving to Raye's kernel asks you to enroll a key on a blue screen, once: Install page.
  • The AMD RX 400 and 500 cards and NVIDIA cards at 144 Hz have fixes I couldn't try on a real card. If the screen stays black, pick “Safe graphics” on the stick.
  • .exe programs: the first launch takes a few seconds, and not all of them work.
  • Offline, Settings say that updates couldn't be checked.
