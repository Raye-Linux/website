The project

Questions

The ones I'd ask coming from Windows.

Is it free?

Yes. Raye costs nothing to download or use, and there are no ads in it.

Is it Windows?

No. Raye keeps the Windows 11 layout so your habits work, but it's a different system. Windows programs go through a compatibility layer, with the limits described on the Windows programs page.

What is it built on?

Raye Linux is based on Ubuntu 26.04 LTS, which provides years of security fixes for the base system and drivers for most PCs. The desktop, the apps, the installer and the updates on top are Raye's, and Raye keeps them up to date. Raye is not affiliated with Canonical or endorsed by it.

Can I try it without installing?

Yes, and that's what the USB stick does by default: it starts on “Try Raye Linux without installing”. Raye runs from the stick and touches neither your PC's disk nor its swap. The Install icon is on the desktop when you want to go on to the install.

Will my programs work?

An .exe starts with a double-click. On its first start, Raye gives it what programs often ask for (.NET 6 to 9, Visual Basic 6, MFC, OpenAL, PhysX) by itself. The Store has Windows programs installed and tried one by one, and says which ones only partly work, such as FL Studio and Affinity. Discord, Steam, VLC, GIMP or LibreOffice exist as Raye versions, in the Store.

What about my games?

Steam and Heroic are in the Store, and Game Mode turns on by itself while you play. Roblox runs through Sober. Games with an anti-cheat in the Windows kernel, such as Valorant, don't work. Easy Anti-Cheat v2 waits for Raye's key to be accepted by Epic: it's all on the Games page.

What is that blue screen at restart?

If Secure Boot is on and you move to Raye's kernel, your PC's firmware asks once to enroll Raye's key. Raye shows you a six-digit code beforehand: press a key within 10 seconds, choose Enroll MOK, Continue, Yes, type the code, then Reboot. If you miss the screen, no harm done. See the Install page.

Is there another desktop besides the one that looks like Windows?

Yes: Hyprland, where windows line up side by side and you do almost everything with the keyboard. Both are in the same install. See Two desktops.

Can I keep Windows?

Yes, Raye can go alongside it. If Windows is encrypted by BitLocker, you first have to turn that encryption off in Windows. Read the warning on the Install page, and back up your files.

What about my Windows files?

If Windows is alongside, its partition shows in This PC under the name Windows, and after the first sign-in Raye offers Settings > Accounts > Files from Windows to copy your folders, wallpaper and favorites. If Raye replaces Windows, “Replace Windows and keep my files” keeps your folders, wallpaper, Wi-Fi networks and browser profile.

Can I have the Windows 10 look?

Yes: Settings > Personalization > Desktop style > Windows 10 style. The taskbar moves to the left with a search box, corners become square and Start gets an app list with tiles.

How much memory does Raye use?

850 MB once you've signed in, measured on the test machine, which has no graphics card. On a real PC the figure will differ, and it will be on the Hardware page. 8 GB is recommended.

What about an older PC?

Raye starts in UEFI, and the stick also starts in the older BIOS mode: I saw it in a 2 GB virtual machine. If the processor is old, the installer says so on its first page, without stopping: some apps and games need a newer one. No real old PC has been tried yet: it's all on the Older PCs page.

My laptop doesn't wake from sleep.

Raye notices: the first time it warns you, the second time in a row it turns sleep off, and closing the lid locks the PC and turns the screen off. Settings > System > Power & battery turns it back on.

Why is there no C: drive?

Because a letter says nothing about what's on a disk. The system disk is called Raye, a Windows partition is called Windows.

Will an update restart my PC while I work?

No. The ones that don't need a restart install while you work; the others wait until you choose “Update and restart”. See Updates.

What doesn't Raye do?

No voice typing, no RGB lighting, no wireless casting to a screen, no Quick Assist, no live captions, no find-my-device, no family accounts or backup to the Windows cloud. The handbook says, for each, what you can do instead.

Does Raye send data about me?

No: no usage reports and no crash reports, and no ads. Like any download, the update servers see your PC's IP address and what is downloaded (Raye Update, the Store, Firefox). The detail, with the durations, is on the Privacy page, and the cookies page says what the site keeps in your browser.

Do I need a Raye account?

No, it's optional: Raye works the same without one. A Raye account, open since 1.0 on account.rayelinux.com, keeps a list of settings, your picture and your app library from one PC to another; never your passwords or your files. It is also how you enter Raye Community and unlock the Beta and Dev channels of Raye Insider.

Who makes Raye?

Me, techoraye, in Quebec. There's more on the About page.

How do I report a problem?

On the Discord server, in #bugs, or at bonjour@rayelinux.com: which PC you have, what you did, what you expected and what happened. A photo of the screen helps a lot.
