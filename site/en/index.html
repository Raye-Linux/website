What's in it

What already works in the beta. The rest gets polished before 1.0.

  • .exe programs

    .exe installers open with a double-click, and the program lands in the Start menu like any other app. Not all of them run yet, and the first start takes a few seconds.

  • Updates that don't interrupt you

    Most install while you work, with no restart. The ones that touch the system itself, like the kernel or drivers, wait until you choose to restart.

  • Secure Boot and encryption

    Raye starts on a PC with Secure Boot turned on. If you encrypt the disk, the TPM chip unlocks it at startup on your PC; put it in another one and it can't be read.

  • In English and French

    Start, Settings, Files and the Store are already translated. You pick the language when you install.

A word

I'm making Raye so that switching systems doesn't mean learning your computer all over again. The taskbar, Start and Settings should be where your hand already goes, and when they aren't, that's a bug.

The public beta came out on November 14. It still has rough edges, and that's exactly why it exists. If something's off on your PC, say it on the Discord, I read everything.

Questions

Who makes Raye Linux?

One person, techoraye, in Québec. I started Raye in 2026 because I wanted a Linux you can pick up without relearning everything. The public beta came out on November 14, 2026. The easiest way to reach me is the Discord server, or bonjour@rayelinux.com.

Is it free?

Yes. Raye Linux costs nothing to download or use, and there are no ads in it.

What is it built on?

Raye Linux is based on Ubuntu 26.04 LTS. That gives it years of security fixes and drivers for most PCs. The desktop, the apps and the settings on top are ours.

Will my programs still work?

Many .exe programs install and open with a double-click, and the Store has a good share of the usual free apps. Some programs don't run yet. If one you need is missing, send me its name: that's how I decide what to fix first.

What kind of PC do I need?

A 64-bit Intel or AMD PC that starts in UEFI mode, with at least 4 GB of memory. Once started, with nothing open, Raye uses about 1.2 GB of it (measured with two screens): the rest is for your apps, and 8 GB is better for lots of tabs or games. The full list is on the download page.

What about 1.0?

It comes after the beta, in one to two weeks. Leave your email if you want me to write you the day it's out.
