Early updates

Raye Insider

Raye Insider gives you new features before everyone else, in exchange for your feedback. You pick it in Settings, and you can go back to Stable whenever you want.

The four channels

Stable
Everyone's channel, picked by default: tested updates.
Public Beta
New features a few weeks early, once they work well. Open to everyone, no account needed.
Beta
Features while we finish them: expect small problems. For Raye accounts accepted as testers.
Dev
Every change as soon as it's made, for people who build Raye. Things can break. For Raye accounts accepted for Dev.

How to join

  1. Public Beta

    Sign up on the Public beta tester page if you want the Discord channel, then open Settings › Raye Update › Raye Insider and pick Public Beta. Picking it is enough to get the updates.

  2. Beta or Dev

    Apply on the Beta tester or Beta tester Dev page. A person of the team reads it. Once you're accepted, sign this PC in to your account in Settings › Accounts: the channel unlocks in Raye Insider.

Leaving

Pick Stable in Raye Insider. Nothing is taken off your PC: the newer versions you got stay until Stable catches up, then they update from Stable as usual. Going back by force could break the system, so Raye doesn't do it.

What is still missing

  • The Beta and Dev channels unlock from your Raye account's badges: without an account signed in on this PC, or without the badge, they stay closed in Settings.
  • The channels' updates are published in the same place as Stable's: the channel is a choice, not a secret.
