The project

What comes after V1

About one version every two weeks. The dates are targets, not promises: when something takes longer than planned, it moves to the next version. The full, up-to-date list is on the Roadmap page.

1.1, early December

  • Raye Community complete: images and videos, private messages, tools for the team, then it replaces the Discord server.
  • Raye in every language.
  • Raye Insider, in Settings: the public beta (sign up on the website) and the Beta and Dev channels, by application, tied to your Raye account. You can leave anytime.
  • More than 40 popular Windows programs in the Store, each one tried, with an “Optimized for Raye” badge.
  • Developer options and a remote web page.
  • Accounts: badges, community and an admin panel.
  • A second edition of Raye, kept in step with this one.

Later

  • The Budgie desktop as another choice.
  • More desktops to choose from, GNOME and niri, with a Windows look.

What is still missing

  • None of this page is done: all of it is planned, some lines are started. I remove or move them rather than leave them sitting without saying so.
